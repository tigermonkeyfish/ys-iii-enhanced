- Hardware: three targets were meant. Krikzz Turbo EverDrive Pro on a TurboGrafx-16: tested on this version's disc on 2026-11-05 by one person ("The patch works on the everdrive the parallax is good."); Continue after power-off and a fresh load were done and the parallax was good; the crowd scene was not watched there. Real CD: a burned CD-R on a PC Engine Duo-RX (built-in drive, System Card 3.0), same day, same person: saved, powered off, reloaded, parallax good, and the crowd conversation seemed correct. The first burn of this disc (Linux, cdrdao, from the bin/cue) did not load; a second burn with ImgBurn on Windows did; this is one case, not a claim about either tool. MiSTer: not tested. Not reported by the tester: the first entry from the opening, the street after a death, the rest of the sheet and the feel of the longer black screens. Not tested: other Duo models, real older System Cards, bosses, confirmed kills. Saving and loading, on Geargrafx only and on this disc: the save is byte for byte the same as the original's; all four loads (each game's save on each game, from a fresh boot) came back at camera 1104 with HP 20; the camera 150 frames after the load is 2 pixels ahead of the original's in the same session and 4 pixels behind from a fresh boot with Continue (see the Load difference in README.md).

Requires a Super System Card 3.0 or a Duo. The disc tests for that card's RAM at boot and stops with a message if it is absent: seen only with a 3.0 card's RAM switched off, on two emulators.
